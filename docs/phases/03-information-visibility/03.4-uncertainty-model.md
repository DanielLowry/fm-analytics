# 03.4 — Uncertainty model

## Goal

Give analytics code principled operations over incomplete observations without
pretending they are exact.

## Work

1. Retain exact/range/unknown values and observation age.
2. Define legal operations such as bounds, interval comparison, and scenario
   sampling outside FMBridge.
3. Require an explicit policy whenever a scalar estimate is needed.
4. Preserve the policy name/version with derived scores.
5. Define conservative, midpoint, optimistic, and distributional baselines.
6. Ensure missingness can itself carry information without treating hidden
   truth as a label.

## Required examples

- comparing a known 13 with a range of 12–15;
- scoring a role when one important attribute is unknown;
- showing best/worst score bounds for a partially scouted player;
- preventing a player with less knowledge from gaining an artificial advantage;
- updating a prior range after a new scout report.

## Exit criteria

- Domain APIs do not offer an implicit `float(observation)` conversion.
- Squad/recruitment designs state which uncertainty policy they use.
- Derived outputs can explain their sensitivity to unknown inputs.
